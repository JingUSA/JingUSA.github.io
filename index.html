Welcome to my page!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science at The University of Texas at Dallas. I also serve as an Editorial Assistant for Political Analysis, assisting with manuscript processing, reviewer identification, and computational replication checks.

My research focuses on comparative politics, digital politics, and political methodology, particularly artificial intelligence (AI), cybersecurity, misinformation, public opinion, and political trust.

My current research examines how cyber espionage disclosures shape institutional confidence, how citizens assign responsibility for harmful AI systems, and how national AI strategies influence subsequent government policymaking.

Methodologically, I use causal inference, survey experiments, computational social science, and quantitative methods.
