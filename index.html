Welcome to my page!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science at The University of Texas at Dallas.

My research lies at the intersection of comparative politics, digital politics, and political methodology, with a particular focus on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, misinformation, public opinion, and political trust.

My current research examines how digital technologies, information environments, and cyber events shape political attitudes, institutional trust, and government behavior across countries.

Methodologically, I use causal inference, survey experiments, computational social science, and quantitative methods.
